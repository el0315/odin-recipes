Apple Pie

Home

Description

My favorite meal after lasagna. Best served with vanilla ice cream.

Ingredients

  • Granny Smith apples
  • Pie crust
  • Brown sugar
  • Optional: vanilla ice cream

Recipe instructions

  1. Cut and peel apples
  2. Fill pie crust
  3. Add brown sugar
  4. Bake at 400 F for 1 hour
